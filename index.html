    What this is

    Saurashtra is a small community language with very little software support. This toolkit gives you a keyboard, a converter between Latin letters, Saurashtra script and Tamil script, a searchable dictionary and a simple Tamil ↔ Saurashtra word lookup. It is free, open source and works offline once loaded.

    ka kaa ki kii ku kuu ke kee kai ko koo kauA vowel after a consonant becomes a vowel sign. a is built in.
    k on its ownA consonant with no vowel gets a virama.
    kh gh chh jh th dh ph bhAspirated letters.
    T Th D Dh N L ShRetroflex letters. Turn on “Capitals are retroflex”, or type .t .th .d .dh .n .l .s or ṭ ḍ ṇ ḷ ṣ.
    N^ ~n or ṅ ñThe letters ṅ and ñ.
    .m .h m~Anusvara, visarga, candrabindu.
    _Splits letters that would otherwise join: k_ha is k + ha, ka_i is ka + i.
    0–9 and |Saurashtra digits and the danda.

    Good to know

    • Converting to Tamil script loses detail: Tamil has no separate letters for k / kh / g / gh. The Convert tab tells you when this happens.
    • Typing directly in the Type tab works best with a computer keyboard. On a phone, use the on-screen keys or the Convert tab, because predictive keyboards interfere.
    • You need a font that includes the Saurashtra block (Noto Sans Saurashtra). The site loads it automatically.
    • Dictionary entries marked draft have not yet been confirmed by a Saurashtra speaker.
